Alejandro, 9-nov-2026: "esta app tiene que ser muy gamey, que dé gustito hacer cosas". Progress bars came first (Day bar, Main Quest, side quests, Actions per piece, with fill animation and glow). Levels and experience come next.

**Why:** the app is how he plays his day; finishing things has to feel rewarding, not just be tracked.

**How to apply:** every new feature asks how it rewards progress (fills, celebrations, game vocabulary). Propose levels/XP when the timing fits. Keep Linear as the source of truth: rewards are computed from Linear state, never stored as a copy of it.
